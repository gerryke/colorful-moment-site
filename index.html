常见问题

换了手机，或者重装后，「完整版」不见了？

打开 App 的「设置」，点「恢复购买」，使用当初购买时的 Apple 账户即可恢复。

填色进度会同步到其他设备吗？

不会。进度只保存在当前设备上；删除 App 会同时删除进度。

FAQ

The "Full Version" is gone after changing or reinstalling?

Open Settings in the App and tap "Restore Purchases" while signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

Does progress sync across devices?

No. Progress stays on the current device, and deleting the App deletes it.
