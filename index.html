常见问题

如何解锁全部作品？

每个主题画廊的前三幅作品可以免费填色，每天可用 3 次提示。一次购买「解锁全部作品」，即可打开所有作品，并将每天的提示额度提高到 30 次。点开任意带锁作品，或进入 App 的「设置」，即可查看价格和购买。

换了手机或重装后，怎样恢复购买？

打开 App 的「设置」，点「恢复购买」，使用当初购买时的 Apple 账户即可恢复。

填色进度会同步到其他设备吗？

不会。进度只保存在当前设备上；删除 App 会同时删除进度。

FAQ

How do I unlock all artwork?

The first three artworks in each themed gallery are free to color, with 3 hints per day. A one-time “Unlock All Artwork” purchase opens every artwork and raises the daily hint allowance to 30. Tap a locked artwork or open Settings to see the price and purchase.

How do I restore my purchase after switching devices or reinstalling?

Open Settings in the App and tap "Restore Purchases" while signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

Does progress sync across devices?

No. Progress stays on the current device, and deleting the App deletes it.
