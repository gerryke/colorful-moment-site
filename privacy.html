和颜时刻 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 5 日

「和颜时刻」（Colorful Moment，以下简称「本 App」）由个人开发者 Gerry Ke 开发。本政策说明本 App 如何处理与你有关的信息。

我们自己不收集个人信息

  • 本 App 不需要注册或登录，没有账号系统。
  • 我们不收集你的姓名、邮箱、电话、通讯录、照片等个人信息，也不使用任何数据分析或统计工具。
  • 本 App 中的广告由 Google 提供，Google 处理数据的方式见下方「广告」一节。

保存在你设备上的数据

你的填色进度、完成的作品、解锁的作品、每日提示次数，以及语言、静音等偏好设置，只保存在你自己的设备上，不会上传到我们的服务器。删除本 App 会同时删除这些数据。

广告

本 App 免费提供，并通过 Google AdMob 展示广告：完成作品后偶尔出现的全屏广告，以及你主动选择观看、用来解锁作品或获得提示的视频广告。填色过程中不会出现广告。

为了展示广告、统计广告效果和防止作弊，Google 可能会收集和处理以下信息：

  • IP 地址，以及由它推断出的大致位置（国家或城市级别）；
  • 设备型号、系统版本、语言等设备与 App 信息；
  • 广告的展示、点击等互动数据，以及诊断数据；
  • 广告标识符（IDFA）：只有在你允许跟踪时才会使用。

跟踪的选择权在你。第一次看到广告前，系统会询问是否允许本 App 跟踪你在其他公司的 App 和网站上的活动。允许后，广告会更贴近你的喜好；不允许也完全不影响使用，你仍然会看到广告，只是它们不会根据你的兴趣投放。你随时可以在 iPhone 的「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪」中修改。

如果你在欧洲经济区、英国或瑞士，第一次看到广告前，还会看到 Google 提供的同意选项；之后可以在本 App 的「设置 → 广告隐私选项」中随时修改。

购买「完整版」后，本 App 不再展示任何广告。Google 如何使用这些数据，请参阅 Google 在使用合作伙伴网站或应用时如何使用数据。

内购

「完整版」等内购通过 Apple 的 App Store 完成。付款与账单信息由 Apple 处理，我们看不到也不会保存你的支付信息。本 App 只会从 Apple 获知某项购买是否有效，用来开启对应功能。Apple 如何处理你的信息，请参阅 Apple 隐私政策。

儿童

本 App 并非专为 13 岁以下儿童设计。我们不会有意收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

政策变更

本政策如有变更，我们会更新本页面并修改上方的生效日期。

Colorful Moment Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

Colorful Moment ("the App") is developed by Gerry Ke, an independent developer. This policy explains how the App handles information about you.

We do not collect personal information ourselves

  • The App has no accounts and needs no sign-up or sign-in.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or other personal information, and we use no analytics tools.
  • Ads in the App are provided by Google; how Google handles data is described under "Advertising" below.

Data kept on your device

Your coloring progress, finished and unlocked artworks, daily hint count and preferences such as language and sound stay on your own device and are never uploaded to our servers. Deleting the App deletes this data.

Advertising

The App is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: an occasional full-screen ad after finishing an artwork, and videos you choose to watch to unlock an artwork or get hints. No ad ever appears while you are coloring.

To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it (country or city level);
  • device and app information such as device model, OS version and language;
  • ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks, and diagnostics;
  • the advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking.

Tracking is your choice. Before the first ad, iOS asks whether the App may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. Allowing it makes ads closer to your taste; declining changes nothing else: you still see ads, just not based on your interests. You can change this anytime in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland you will also see Google's consent choices before the first ad, and you can change them anytime in the App under Settings → Ad Privacy Options.

After buying the "Full Version", the App shows no ads at all. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

Purchases such as the "Full Version" are made through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment and billing; we never see or store your payment details. The App only learns from Apple whether a purchase is valid, to unlock the matching feature. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact
