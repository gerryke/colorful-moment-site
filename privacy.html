和颜 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 4 日

「和颜」（Colorful Moment，以下简称「本 App」）由个人开发者 Gerry Ke 开发。本政策说明本 App 如何处理与你有关的信息。

我们自己不收集个人信息

  • 本 App 不需要注册或登录，没有账号系统。
  • 我们不会通过本 App 主动收集你的姓名、邮箱、电话、通讯录或照片，也没有自建分析系统或单独接入行为分析服务。Google 广告 SDK 的数据处理见下方「广告」一节。
  • 本 App 中的广告由 Google 提供，Google 处理数据的方式见下方「广告」一节。

保存在你设备上的数据

你的填色进度、完成的作品、解锁的作品、每日提示次数，以及语言、静音等偏好设置，只保存在你自己的设备上，不会上传到我们的服务器。删除本 App 会同时删除这些数据。

广告

本 App 通过 Google AdMob 展示广告：完成作品后偶尔出现的全屏广告，以及你主动选择观看、用来解锁作品或获得提示的视频广告。填色过程中不会出现广告。

为了展示广告、统计广告效果和防止作弊，Google 可能会收集和处理以下信息：

  • IP 地址，以及由它推断出的大致位置（国家或城市级别）；
  • 设备型号、系统版本、语言等设备与 App 信息；
  • 广告的展示、点击等互动数据，以及诊断数据；
  • 其他由 Google SDK 处理的 App 或开发者范围内的设备标识，以及 App 启动、视频观看等使用互动数据；不包含本 App 请求或读取的 IDFA。

ATT 与 IDFA（自 1.0 Build 9 起）：本 App 不请求 App Tracking Transparency（ATT）授权，不显示 ATT 授权弹窗或其说明页，也不请求、读取或使用广告标识符 IDFA。我们通过 Google Mobile Ads SDK 的发布商隐私设置关闭广告个性化。这不代表 Google SDK 完全不收集或处理数据；上述广告、设备标识及诊断数据仍可能由 Google 处理。广告解锁及提示奖励不以同意追踪为条件。

如果你在欧洲经济区、英国或瑞士等适用地区，Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）会在需要时显示区域广告同意选项，并在需要时提供本 App「设置 → 广告隐私选项」入口。这类区域同意选项与 ATT 系统追踪授权不同。

购买「去广告」后，本 App 不再展示全屏广告；提示用完时，你仍可以自行选择观看视频来获得更多提示。Google 如何使用这些数据，请参阅 Google 在使用合作伙伴网站或应用时如何使用数据。

内购

「去广告」等内购通过 Apple 的 App Store 完成。付款与账单信息由 Apple 处理，我们看不到也不会保存你的支付信息。本 App 只会从 Apple 获知某项购买是否有效，用来开启对应功能。Apple 如何处理你的信息，请参阅 Apple 隐私政策。

儿童

本 App 并非专为 13 岁以下儿童设计。我们不会有意收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

政策变更

本政策如有变更，我们会更新本页面并修改上方的生效日期。

Colorful Moment Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

Colorful Moment ("the App") is developed by Gerry Ke, an independent developer. This policy explains how the App handles information about you.

We do not collect personal information ourselves

  • The App has no accounts and needs no sign-up or sign-in.
  • We do not actively collect your name, email, phone number, contacts or photos through the App, and we have no developer-operated analytics system or separate behavioral analytics integration. Data processing by Google's advertising SDK is described below.
  • Ads in the App are provided by Google; how Google handles data is described under "Advertising" below.

Data kept on your device

Your coloring progress, finished and unlocked artworks, daily hint count and preferences such as language and sound stay on your own device and are never uploaded to our servers. Deleting the App deletes this data.

Advertising

The App shows ads through Google AdMob: an occasional full-screen ad after finishing an artwork, and videos you choose to watch to unlock an artwork or get hints. No ad ever appears while you are coloring.

To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it (country or city level);
  • device and app information such as device model, OS version and language;
  • ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks, and diagnostics;
  • other app- or developer-scoped device identifiers processed by the Google SDK, and usage interactions such as app launches and video views; the App does not request or read IDFA.

ATT and IDFA (from version 1.0, Build 9): The App does not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) authorization, display the ATT permission prompt or a tracking explanation screen, or request, read or use the advertising identifier IDFA. We disable ad personalization through the Google Mobile Ads SDK publisher privacy setting. This does not mean that the Google SDK collects or processes no data; Google may still process the advertising, device identifier and diagnostic data described above. Ad unlocks and hint rewards are not conditional on consenting to tracking.

In applicable regions, including the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) presents regional advertising consent choices when required and provides Settings → Ad Privacy Options in the App when required. These regional choices are separate from the iOS ATT tracking permission.

After buying "Remove Ads", the App shows no more full-screen ads; when you run out of hints, you can still choose to watch a video for more. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

Purchases such as "Remove Ads" are made through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment and billing; we never see or store your payment details. The App only learns from Apple whether a purchase is valid, to unlock the matching feature. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
